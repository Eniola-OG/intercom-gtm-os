# Brand: tone of voice and key phrases

Taken from Fin's and Intercom's own copy in `context/docs/research/voice.md` (tags V1 to V12), checked 2026-11-12. Phrases are exact. Tone descriptions are my reading (INFERRED) of that copy.

## Two names, two registers

| Name | What it is | Tone in its copy (INFERRED) |
|---|---|---|
| **Fin** | The company and the AI agent: "the Customer Agent company" (V1) | Ambitious, superlative, AI-first: "The highest performing Customer Agent" (V2) |
| **Intercom** | The helpdesk product: "Intercom will live on as our customer service software platform" (V5) | Calmer, about the whole system: "A complete system for AI and human customer service" (V3) |

## Tone of voice (INFERRED from voice.md section 7)

| Trait | Shows up as |
|---|---|
| **Confident to combative** | "provably superior technology"; "baggage be gone." (V5) |
| **Superlative** | "the highest-performing AI agent" (V3); "best-in-market results" (V2) |
| **Proof-led** | A big aggregate number, then named logos: "averaging 76% across 12,000+ customers" (V2) |
| **Category-making** | "this new customer agent category" (V5); "Fin invented and championed outcome based pricing" (V2) |
| **Simple, declarative headings** | "Fin gets better every week" (V2); "Self-improving system" (V3) |

## Key phrases (exact)

**Who they are**
- "Fin is the Customer Agent company delivering perfect customer experiences – from support to sales to commerce." (V1)
- "Perfect customer experiences made possible with Fin" (V2)
- "Intercom is the only helpdesk with a natively integrated AI Agent, Fin, meaning every customer conversation improves the next one, enabling perfect experiences that were never possible before." (V3)
- "internet business should feel personal." (V1)

**What Fin does**
- "Fin works seamlessly with every helpdesk" (V2)
- "Fin can resolve every customer issue, no matter how complex" (V2)
- "Fin learns from every interaction and continuously improves" (V2)
- "Fin speaks dozens of languages, and works 24 hours a day, every day." (V2)

**No migration, no lock-in**
- "No need to replace the systems your team spent years building." (V8)
- "Deploy Fin on any helpdesk over API. No migration required." (V8)
- "Set up in under an hour on your current helpdesk" (V6)

**Pricing**
- "No seats required" (V6)
- "You're not charged when a conversation is simply passed to your team without an outcome." (V7)
- "Transparent pricing" (V3)

## Word choices (from voice.md section 7)

- **Recurring words:** "perfect", "highest performing", "outcome", "resolution", "customer agent".
- **Never in their copy:** "chatbot".

## For our own copy

- Use their category words ("customer agent", "resolution", "outcome") so we sound like the brand.
- Keep the superlatives ("perfect", "highest performing") for quotes attributed to Fin, not for our own claims. INFERRED.
- No em dashes (repo rule, CLAUDE.md). Some of Fin's own quotes contain en dashes. Keep them only inside exact quotes.
