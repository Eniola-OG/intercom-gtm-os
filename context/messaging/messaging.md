# Messaging: Fin (formerly Intercom)

Every claim traces to `context/docs/research/`, cited as file plus source tag. Customer numbers are Fin's published figures. INFERRED marks my reading.

## Core message

**Resolve more of your support volume on the helpdesk you already run, and pay only when Fin delivers an outcome.**

Source: "Deploy Fin on any helpdesk over API. No migration required." (company.md C12); "$0.99 per outcome" and "You're not charged when a conversation is simply passed to your team without an outcome." (company.md C7).

## Message pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof points |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Resolves more** | Fin resolves conversations end to end, and the rate goes up over time | "averaging 76% across 12,000+ customers" (company.md C6). "the average resolution rate increases 1% every month" (voice.md V2). Lightspeed went from 20% to "over 70%" (customers.md K8) |
| **2. No migration** | Fin runs on top of Zendesk, HubSpot, Freshdesk, Salesforce or any helpdesk over API | "keeping Zendesk as your system of record" (company.md C12). Lightspeed runs it on Zendesk (K8). Vanta started on its existing ticketing system (K9) |
| **3. Pay for outcomes, not seats** | $0.99 per outcome, one outcome per conversation, nothing for a plain handoff | "No seats required" (company.md C8). "You're only charged for one outcome per conversation" (company.md C7) |
| **4. Scale without headcount growing at the same pace** | Volume grows while the team stays lean | Synthesia: ">275kadditional monthly queries handled with same headcount"; Rocket Money "~$1MAnnual ROI" (customers.md section 2). Birdie Care cut expected headcount "by 30%" (K11) |

## Angle per persona

| Persona | Angle | Lead pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Champion (support leader) | You're hiring to keep up with volume. Fin resolves the repeat questions so the team you have can handle the rest. Proof: Vanta, 71% resolution and 96.7% CSAT (customers.md K9) | 1, then 2 |
| Economic buyer (operations or finance) | Support cost doesn't have to rise with every new customer. Proof: Rocket Money ~$1M annual ROI, solidcore $569k saved (customers.md section 2) | 4, then 3 |
| Influencer (systems or AI owner) | No migration and no in-house build. Proof: Lightspeed "worked with our complexity" (K8); Anthropic bought rather than built (K10) | 2 |
