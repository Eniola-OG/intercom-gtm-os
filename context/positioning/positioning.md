## Positioning statement

For support leaders at product-led software, AI and fintech companies whose user base is growing faster than their support team, Fin is the customer agent that resolves conversations on the helpdesk they already run, with no migration. Teams today meet volume by hiring more agents, relying on their helpdesk's built-in AI, or building their own. Fin publishes an average resolution rate of 76% across more than 12,000 customers and charges $0.99 only when it delivers an outcome. In a head-to-head on Vanta's own conversations, it resolved about 73% against the incumbent's 49%. Since September 2026, Fin has been part of Salesforce (company.md C3, C6, C7, C12; customers.md K9).

## Watch-outs

- Ownership: Fin has been part of Salesforce since 10 Sept 2026 (company.md C3). Rivals pitch that as a risk (competitors.md P17). Don't hide it, and don't lead with it either. INFERRED.
- Every customer number is published by Fin. Quote them as Fin's figures (customers.md caveat).
