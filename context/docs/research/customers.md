## 4. Deep-dive case studies

### Lightspeed [K8]
- **Before:** tens of thousands of requests a month, mostly by phone and email, across many languages and time zones. Years of acquisitions left "numerous Salesforce instances, overlapping ERPs, and siloed data."
- **Switched from:** no vendor was replaced. They kept Zendesk for ticketing and multiple Salesforce instances, and had earlier tried building their own AI assistant (vendor not named). Fin went live "within weeks" after an early 2023 beta.
- **Results:**
  - "More than 43,000 customer requests are resolved by AI each month."
  - "88% of all support conversations now involve Fin, with 100% of chat conversations starting with the AI Agent."
  - "72% of Fin conversations are resolved without human intervention."
- **Quotes** (Yamine Gluchow, VP of Information Systems):
  - "We didn't have to untangle everything, Fin worked with our complexity, not in spite of it."
  - "When support volume gets that high, your only option is throwing bodies at the problem."
  - "We started at 20% resolution," ... "Today, we're at over 70%."
- **Copilot quote** (Angelo Livanos, VP, Global Support, Lightspeed Commerce): "Our Lightspeed customer service agents are dramatically more efficient when using Copilot." [K7]

### Vanta [K9]
- **Before:** "a ticketing system with an AI chatbot on top". The AI was "deflecting around 45% of chat conversations", had "stopped improving", and had "no coverage over email, their highest-volume channel".
- **Switched from:** an incumbent ticketing system and AI chatbot (vendor not named). In a head-to-head test on 400 real conversations, Fin resolved about 73% against the incumbent's roughly 49%.
- **Rollout:** Fin on the existing ticketing system first, then a full move to the Intercom helpdesk with 700+ help center articles. Email, Slack and Procedures came next. Fin is branded "Ask Ilma".
- **Results:** "71%Fin resolution rate" ("nearly 2,500 each month"); "40%Above deflection target"; "96.7%CSAT YTD".
- **Quotes:**
  - Margarita Wilshire, Director of Customer Support: "It felt like we had outgrown the setup, and it was only a matter of time before it stopped holding."
  - Margarita Wilshire: "We are shifting from AI adoption to AI orchestration."
  - Kelly Bray, SVP of Global Sales: "I love the idea of a single front door,"

### Anthropic ("Build vs buy") [K10]
- **Before:** support volume that "regularly spike[s]", and a customer base running from free Claude users to enterprise API customers. This was a build-versus-buy decision, so no incumbent vendor is named.
- **Deployed:** Fin with Intercom in the Messenger and email, live "In under a week".
- **Results:** "96% involvement rate"; "50.8% resolution rate", "up from 36% at launch"; "1,700 hours saved in first month".
- **Quotes:**
  - Emily Lampert, Head of Product Support: "The time savings have been huge – in the first month, we'd already saved over 1,700 hours of our team's time,"
  - Emily Lampert: "Intercom's help center and Fin are now key cornerstones for how we support free users at scale."
  - Homepage quote, Isabel Larrow, Head of AI Support: "We've been seeing around 560,000 resolutions a month with Fin, at about a 79% resolution rate..." [K5]

### Birdie Care [K11]
- **Before:** "12 support associates, several managers, and a head of support". Product scope "doubled ... over the last 18 months". Privacy concerns about personal data in chat.
- **Switched from:** Zendesk, and back again. They left Intercom for Zendesk "without a thorough request for proposal (RFP) process, and after a hasty migration", then "Within a week, we pivoted back."
- **Results:** "26%Fin resolution rate, for conversations it was involved in"; "20ppincrease in auto-resolution rates within just two months"; expected headcount reduced "by 30%".
- **Quotes** (Luke Cullimore, credited as "Senior Revenue Operations Lead" in the header and "Revenue Operations Manager" in the body):
  - "We had a brief period where we looked at Zendesk and actually started trying to implement it."
  - "The feedback loop for Fin is probably the most optimal feedback loop we've got around our help center."
