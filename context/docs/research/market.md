## 1. Regulation

### EU AI Act, Article 50: chatbots must disclose they are AI (in force since 2 August 2026)
- The Commission says: "On 2 August 2026, new rules on the transparency of AI systems take effect." [M1]
- "Users must be clearly informed when they are not interacting with a real person", with "a chatbot, AI agent, and avatar" as examples. [M1]
- **Article 50(1)** covers "AI systems intended to interact directly with natural persons". These must be designed so that "the natural persons concerned are informed that they are interacting with an AI system", "unless this is obvious from the point of view of a natural person who is reasonably well-informed, observant and circumspect". [M2]
- **Article 50(5):** the information must be given "at the latest at the time of the first interaction or exposure". [M2]
- **Penalties (Article 99(4)(g)):** "up to EUR 15 000 000 or, if the offender is an undertaking, up to 3 % of its total worldwide annual turnover ... whichever is higher". SMEs pay whichever amount is lower (Article 99(6)). [M3] The Commission news item gives the same figure: "up to €15 million, or 3% of global annual turnover". [M1]
- **Reach:** commentary says the rule applies to any business serving EU users, wherever it is based. (Search) [M4]
- **Provider versus deployer:** one source says the vendor carries the design duty and the website operator must not switch the disclosure off. (Search) [M4]
- **A later deadline:** machine-readable marking of synthetic content under Article 50(2) is reported as deferred to 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market. Sources disagree on this, so it's unverified. (Search) [M4]
- INFERRED:
  - Companies running homegrown or older chatbots for EU users had to add AI disclosure by 2 August 2026.
  - It's a compliance trigger to review the support bot, though it doesn't by itself force a switch of vendor.
  - Fin lists ISO 42001 and AIUC-1 among its certifications [M10], which speaks to AI governance buyers.

### European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882): applies from 28 June 2025
- The Commission page says "Member States had to incorporate the European Accessibility Act into their national law by June 2022." It lists "banking services" and "e-commerce" among the covered services. [M5]
- The **28 June 2025** application date comes from law firm commentary (Taylor Wessing, Bird & Bird), not from the Commission page that was read. (Search) [M6]
- Commentary says customer support channels must give accessibility information in accessible ways, and that support staff need training. (Search) [M6]
- **Transition period:** reported until 28 June 2030 for services using products already in use before June 2025, but its exact scope is unclear. (Search) [M6]
- **Microenterprise exemption** (<10 staff and <€2M revenue): reported only by a vendor source and unverified. (Search) [M6]

### Colorado AI Act (SB 24-205)
- Enacted on 17 May 2024. A deployer of an AI system meant to interact with consumers "must ensure disclosure to each consumer who interacts with the artificial intelligence system that the consumer is interacting with an artificial intelligence system". [M7]
- The original date for developer and deployer duties was 1 February 2026. [M7]
- SB25B-004, signed on 28 August 2025, moved the date to **30 June 2026**, according to several law firm notes. (Search) [M8]
- Whether the 2026 legislative session changed it again: **Not found**.
- INFERRED: the consumer-disclosure duty is the part that matters for support bots. The heavier "high-risk" duties are about consequential decisions such as lending and employment, not routine support.

## 2. Market changes

### Analyst forecast
- Gartner press release dated 5 March 2025, titled "Gartner Predicts Agentic AI Will Autonomously Resolve 80% of Common Customer Service Issues Without Human Intervention by 2029". [M9]
  - The fetch of gartner.com failed (HTTP 403). Only the title was seen, via search.
  - Secondary coverage adds "a 30% reduction in operational costs". Unverified. (Search) [M9]

### Vendor survey data (Intercom's own report, so weigh accordingly)
- 2026 Customer Service Transformation Report, about 2,400 support professionals surveyed. All figures read via search only. [M11]
  - "82% of senior leaders say their teams invested in AI for customer service over the last 12 months, with 87% planning to invest in 2026."
  - "Across all respondents, 62% say their customer service metrics have improved since implementing AI."
  - "improving customer experience has become the top priority for 2026 – cited by 58% of all teams, up from just 28% last year."
  - "52% of organizations are planning to scale AI beyond support in 2026"
  - New roles are appearing: "conversation analysts, knowledge managers, AI ops".
  - About 10% reach "mature deployment". This comes from a third-party listing, not Intercom's own pages, so it's unverified.

### Consolidation
- **Salesforce bought Fin** for about $3.6B. Announced 15 June 2026, closed 10 September 2026. [M12] [M13]
- **Zendesk bought Forethought:** "A new chapter begins: Forethought is now part of Zendesk." [M14]
- **Salesforce bought Qualified**, reported 1 April 2026. Mentioned in one search result only, unverified. [M15]
- INFERRED:
  - The AI support category is consolidating into the big CRM and helpdesk platforms.
  - Customers of an acquired vendor tend to re-evaluate (see the "switch from Fin" pages in competitors.md).
  - The same goes for Fin customers who run Zendesk, HubSpot or Freshdesk and now buy their AI from a Salesforce company.

### Pricing shift to outcomes
- Fin: "$0.99 per outcome". [M10]
- Zendesk: pricing "based on the successful outcomes they deliver". [M14]
- Sierra: "Pay for a job well done." [M14]
- HubSpot: "$0.50" per resolution. [M14]
- INFERRED: buyers can now compare price per resolution directly, which pushes the price per resolution down (HubSpot says it is "half the price of leading competitors").

### AI agents moving beyond support
- Fin for Sales launched in April 2026, priced at $9.99 per qualification. (Search) [M15]
- Fin covers "Service / Sales / Ecommerce / Success" roles. [M10]
