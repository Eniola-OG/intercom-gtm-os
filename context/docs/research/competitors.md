# Competitors: who else sells to Intercom / Fin buyers

Raw research, not conclusions. Public sources only. Source tags like [P1] map to the table at the bottom (URL, date checked, how read).
- All homepages were checked on 2026-11-12. Quotes are exact but some were cut to 125 characters ("..." marks a cut).
- Stats are each vendor's own claims, copied exactly and not verified.
- INFERRED = my reading.

## 1. How the market splits (from third-party sources)

- A vendor-written comparison splits the field into two camps. Agentforce, Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI assume you live inside their own platform to get full value. Decagon, Sierra and Ada sit on top of an existing helpdesk. (Search) [P13]
- Fin's own position straddles both camps: it has a native helpdesk (Intercom) and also says "Deploy Fin on any helpdesk over API. No migration required." [P12]
- **Since 10 September 2026, Fin is part of Salesforce** [P14], which also sells Agentforce. INFERRED: Agentforce is now a sibling product, not a competitor in the usual sense. The two overlap in the market, and Salesforce describes Fin as aimed at "SMB and some commercial organizations". [P15]
- **Forethought is now owned by Zendesk:** "A new chapter begins: Forethought is now part of Zendesk." [P6]

## 3. How each one positions itself (exact quotes)

### Zendesk [P1]
- "AI that gets smarter with every resolution."
- "Launch in minutes, not months"
- "Orchestrate actions across any system"
- "Govern with built-in controls and testing"
- Logos: Siemens, GitHub, Uber, Box, Vimeo, Tesco, Lush, Discord.
- Its own customer counts don't match: "32,000+ customers" on the homepage and "22,000+ service teams" on the AI agents page.

### Salesforce Agentforce [P2]
- "Lose the rigid chatbots and hold times."
- Extend agents "On the web, on the phone, or in apps."
- Claims G2 "#1 Leader" for AI Agents.
- Logos: OpenTable, SharkNinja, Indeed, Heathrow, Equinox, Finnair, Prudential.
- Agentforce "reached $1.2 billion in ARR in Q1 FY27, up 205% year-over-year." [P15]

### Decagon [P3]
- "Build, optimize, and scale AI agents that treat every customer like the only one."
- "Build once. Deploy everywhere."
- Workflows ("AOPs") defined "in natural language".
- Logos: Deutsche Telekom, American Airlines, Square, Mercado Libre, Figma, Duolingo, Notion, Chime.

### Sierra [P4]
- "The leading conversational AI platform for businesses."
- "Deploy a single agent across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice, and ChatGPT."
- "Build powerful AI agents quickly—with or without engineering support."
- Logos: Rocket Mortgage, SiriusXM, Uber, Vanguard, Sutter Health, Redfin, Wayfair.

### Ada [P5]
- "Elevate your CX with AI agents proven to outperform humans"
- "Orchestrate multiple LLMs to understand, act, and resolve complex inquiries."
- Recently announced "Ada with Dialpad".
- Logos: Pinterest, monday.com, Sky, Barnes & Noble, Grab, GoFundMe.

### Forethought [P6]
- AI agents "reason, decide, and take action using your business policies."
- Product pillars: "Fully Agentic," "Trained on Your Data," "Multi-Agent System."
- Logos: Grammarly, Upwork, Thumbtack, UPS, WordPress.com.

### Gorgias [P7]
- "AI Agent + Helpdesk, built as one."
- "Pre-trained on 1B+ conversations."
- "Your rules. Your AI."
- Logos: Dr. Bronner's, bareMinerals, Arc'teryx, Orthofeet.

### Kustomer [P8]
- "Kustomer's AI-native platform is built for the full customer relationship."
- "Set a business goal and every AI action orients toward it."
- Logos: sweetgreen, Turo, Skims, Rappi, Everlane.

### Freshworks Freshdesk [P9]
- "Deploy OOTB AI agents built for real support use cases."
- "Free AI onboarding included."
- Logos and trust bar: Bridgestone, PepsiCo, iPostal1, Hobbycraft.

### HubSpot [P10]
- "Scale support without adding headcount."
- "Customer agent is available at half the price of leading competitors"
- Logos: babbel, MYOB, Kahoot, Weightwatchers, monday.com.
- INFERRED: "half the price" lines up with $0.50 against Fin's $0.99.

## 4. How Fin positions against them

- "Fin is the highest performing customer agent on the market" [P0]
- "Fin works seamlessly with every helpdesk" [P0]
- "Fin invented and championed outcome based pricing" [P0]
- Fin claims G2 leadership "with a 90% satisfaction score, beating the nearest competitor by 8 percentage points" [P0]
- Benchmark against Anthropic's model: "+15%" "Confirmed resolutions vs Sonnet 5" [P0]
- CEO's view of rivals, from the rebrand post: "the relative success of the newcomers in our category, despite the fact that we have provably superior technology" [P11]
- Fin publishes "alternatives" and "vs" pages, for example "Top Agentforce Alternatives & Competitors to Try in 2026" (Search). [P16]

## 5. Competitors' moves since the Salesforce deal

- Rival vendors publish "switch from Fin" pages that pitch independence from Salesforce and a choice of model. These are vendor marketing, read via search only. [P17]
  - Bland: "Fin is getting acquired. Is now the time to switch?"
  - Plain: "Intercom alternatives" after the Salesforce acquisition.
  - Voiceflow: "Fin alternatives".
- A commentary piece says the main customer worry is roadmap independence and the product drifting toward Agentforce. (Search, dragapp.com) [P18]
- Specific switching discounts from Zendesk or HubSpot aimed at Fin customers: **Not found**.
