## 7. Patterns in the language (INFERRED)

- **Words they use over and over:** "perfect" (customer experiences), "highest performing", "outcome", "resolution", "customer agent". They say "customer agent", not "chatbot". The word "chatbot" doesn't appear in their own copy that was read.
- **Category framing:** they claim to have created a category: "this new customer agent category", "Fin invented and championed outcome based pricing".
- **Tone:** confident, superlative and combative ("provably superior technology", "baggage be gone", "destroying your past").
- **Two names:** Fin sounds like an ambitious AI company, while Intercom copy is calmer and talks about the system ("complete system", "seamless", "works with the tools you already use").
- **Proof style:** a big aggregate number (76%, 12,000+, 2 million weekly), backed by many named logos with exact metrics.
