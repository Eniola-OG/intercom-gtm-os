# Case studies: Fin and Intercom customers

Every story found in `context/docs/research/customers.md`, which was checked 2026-11-12.
- Results and quotes are exact and published by Fin.
- "Not stated" means the story page didn't say. "Not in research" means it wasn't recorded.
- "Attribution unclear" means the speaker was credited only by where they appear on the page layout.
